One claim. Two compounding risks.

The Pennington Law homepage advertises "more than 60 years of helping Arizona." Andre Pennington passed the Arizona bar in 2016. Pennington Law was not a 60-year-old practice when this was written.

Risk 1: AZ ER 7.1 legal-advertising compliance. The State Bar of Arizona prohibits false or misleading communication about an attorney's services. The "60 years" claim, as written, is a direct exposure surface for an ER 7.1 complaint. Filing an ER 7.1 complaint takes 5 minutes. Defending one costs days of attorney time and creates a public record.

Risk 2: HNW prospect trust collapse. Estate-planning prospects do their own research. The moment a prospect cross-references "60 years" against the bar admission date, on-page trust signals collapse. CTR suppression and consultation drop-off follow.

Time to fix: 5 minutes. We do it on Day 1, before any other work begins. The replacement language ("Decades of combined trust, tax, and estate experience" or specific years for Andre + Christopher Baker + Anthony Coggin combined) preserves the trust value without the legal exposure.

05Common Questions

What people actually ask.

Who is Andre Pennington?

+

Andre L. Pennington, Esq. is a Wealth Attorney® and Registered Financial Planner. He founded Pennington Law, PLLC in Arizona (estate planning, asset protection, probate) and serves as CEO of Universal Wealth, LLC, a national financial planning network of approximately 1,500 advisors. He's an Inc. 5000 CEO (2024), Forbes Finance Council member, and was named in the Wall Street Journal's Most Influential Attorneys list in 2024.

What is Pennington Law?

+

Pennington Law, PLLC is Andre's Arizona-based legal practice founded in 2016, focused on estate planning, asset protection, probate administration, wills and trusts, advance directives, and retirement protection. Offices in Surprise, Sun City West, and Buckeye (Verrado). Hours Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM.

What is Universal Wealth?

+

Universal Wealth, LLC is Andre's national financial planning company founded in 2023. It integrates asset protection, estate planning, tax law, and financial planning into a unified discipline through a network of approximately 1,500 advisors nationwide.

What credentials does Andre hold?

+

Juris Doctor from Loyola Law School (Los Angeles, 2006), B.A. from California State University Fullerton, member of the Arizona State Bar (Probate and Trust Section), Colorado State Bar, U.S. Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces, and the Air Force Court of Criminal Appeals. Major in the U.S. Air Force Reserves and former U.S. Air Force trial attorney (top 5%). Registered Financial Planner® and Wealth Attorney®.
